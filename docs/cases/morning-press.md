# The Dawn Workshop — published test, inadequate artistic result

October 17, 2026. Exploratory test made quickly with **GPT-6 Astra,
`reasoning_effort = high`** for authorship, critique and QA, with explicit model
selection. Neither `max` nor `ultra` was tried for this film. Rendering uses the
local `motion/1` core, without assets generated by another model, remote services
or API keys.

**Final status: artistic failure against the user's request.** The technical
checks below passed, but the user subsequently judged the rendering highly
inadequate, particularly the mechanical logic beneath the lever. The film is
published with its defects. It demonstrates neither studio quality nor a
production-ready result. See [human feedback after delivery](#human-feedback-after-delivery)
and the [test limitations](../LIMITATIONS.md).

A blank sheet enters a press; the platen descends, the ink is revealed as the
sheet exits, then the sun print rests on the table. The four shots total 14 seconds.
The descriptor contains 81 objects and five synthetic sound accents. The film
contains no text.

## Human feedback after delivery

The user subsequently judged this rendering **highly inadequate**, with missing
mechanical logic beneath the lever. They believe Claude would have done much
better. This final human judgment is recorded after the production review;
earlier observations must not be read as artistic acceptance. The review is preserved in English translation. Archived media and manifests retain
their original capture hashes; original source bytes remain recoverable at pinned
historical commits. Current English documents and UI are translations, with no new
render claimed. See [translation provenance](../assets/TRANSLATION_PROVENANCE.md).

The code confirms a specific limitation: `lever.rotation` and `platen.y`/`shaft.y`
are separately animated tracks, synchronized by cues, with different curves.
No kinematic constraint derives platen displacement from lever movement. The
storyboard's intent — the lever drives the screw and platen — is therefore not
guaranteed by the film's construction. Frame repeatability does not demonstrate
the credibility of that relationship.

This test was exploratory and quick. Neither a Claude trial under identical
conditions nor a version of this film using Astra at `max` or `ultra` effort
provides a basis here for comparing models or settings. After this first
production, films lasting four to six seconds and focusing on one action were
proposed as a direction to test, not as a demonstrated capability.
The [limitations summary](../LIMITATIONS.md) clarifies supported conclusions and
unknowns. No new rendering or film improvement was performed for this publication.

### Closeout addendum — October 17, 2026

A subsequent second local trial used Astra `ultra` for authorship, critique and QA,
with six seconds, one shot and one mechanically constrained action. The user also
rejected it for its poor graphics. Its sources and media are unpublished; this
finding remains anonymous. `max` remains untested. The
[closeout report](../EXPERIMENT_REPORT.md) records both results, the user's feedback
on Claude renders seen elsewhere and the decision to make development dormant.
This addendum does not modify the master, its sources or its production review.

## Settings and measurements

The master is 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 14 s, with four subframes per frame and a shutter
of 0.5 frame intervals. Captures are averaged in RGBA sRGB before encoding.
MP4 size: **1,818,063 bytes**.

The WAV contains 672,000 stereo sample frames at 48 kHz, or 14 s. Its measured
peak is −1.00015 dBFS and RMS −30.2834 dBFS, with no clipped samples. The five
synthetic accents derive from descriptor cues. AAC decoding produces 672,768
frames, or 14.016 s: the extra 768 frames are accepted encoding padding. All five
cue windows have a nonzero decoded signal. **LUFS was not measured.**

## Verification and reproduction commands

Chrome and FFmpeg paths can be supplied through `CHROME_PATH` and `FFMPEG_PATH`
or the corresponding options. Film check performed:

```sh
node scripts/verify-film.mjs --project=examples/morning-press/project.json --out=out/morning-press/verify.json --scale=.25
```

To reproduce the master in a new file:

```sh
node scripts/render.mjs --html="/examples/core-demo/index.html?capture=1&project=/examples/morning-press/project.json" --project=examples/morning-press/project.json --samples=4 --out=out/morning-press/rebuild.mp4
```

The delivered poster and preview were produced locally with FFmpeg from the master:

```sh
ffmpeg -ss 12.5 -i out/morning-press/film.mp4 -frames:v 1 -vf scale=1280:-1 -c:v libwebp -quality 90 out/morning-press/poster.webp
ffmpeg -i out/morning-press/film.mp4 -an -vf fps=12,scale=768:-1 -c:v libwebp -quality 75 -compression_level 4 -loop 0 out/morning-press/preview.webp
```

## Limitations

RGBA comparison covers the report's times and resolution on this browser. It is
not universal proof of purity or cross-platform identity. SVG checks concern DOM
and state, not its pixels. Atlases and strips are image observations; the muted
playback test establishes player progression. **No full real-time viewing or human
listening to the sound is claimed.** The sound button was functionally tested,
without perceptual evaluation of the accompaniment.
