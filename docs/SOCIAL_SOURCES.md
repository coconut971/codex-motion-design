## What the implementation can establish

Technical provenance comes from the six source repositories actually cloned and inspected, with commit-specific references and limitations in [SOURCE_AUDIT.md](SOURCE_AUDIT.md). The executable example and its checks provide evidence for our own implementation.

The design decisions include direct evaluation at a requested time, analytical springs, a shared picture/sound timeline, intentional visual constraints, and frame-based review. Their inclusion does not establish that any named social account originated them. In particular, no universal spring preset, fixed beat duration or subjective review score is presented as an author's verified prescription.

Source model names are historical labels from the brief or source material. This project does not rely on claims about those models' release dates, capabilities, performance or ability to replace a professional tool.

## Closing the remaining attribution gap

If a readable primary article or an author-provided export becomes available, record its canonical URL, author, publication date, date accessed and which sections were actually inspected. Link a technical observation only to the primary passage that supports it. Record separately whether a film, its source code, an attached PDF and its sound were inspectable. An accessible article alone does not establish all four.

Preserve rights and attribution for any material actually reused. Public availability is not a redistribution licence. The templates and code in this project are original adaptations; this register does not grant permission to redistribute social-media prompts, PDFs or videos.
